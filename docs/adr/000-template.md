# ADR-000: Template

Status: Template · Date: YYYY-MM-DD

> Copy this file to `NNN-short-slug.md`. Keep it to one page. Write it when the
> decision is made, not at the end of the project — by then the real reason is
> gone and what gets written down is a rationalisation.

## Context

What problem forced a decision? What constraints were in play — the shared
schema, two ORMs, the contract, the deadline? Someone reading this in Phase 5
needs enough to understand why the obvious option was not taken.

## Decision

What was chosen. One or two sentences, in the present tense.

## Alternatives considered

The options that were rejected, and why each was rejected. **This is the
section people actually read** — it is the difference between having chosen
something and having accepted a default.

- **Option A** — rejected because …
- **Option B** — rejected because …

## Consequences

What this buys, what it costs, and what now has to be watched. Include the
consequences that are annoying; an ADR with only upsides has not been thought
through.
